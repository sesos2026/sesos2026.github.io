TOPICS


  • The joint workshop will focus on the following (and similar) themes:

  • SoS: SoS analysis & architecture (requirements engineering, architectural design, evaluation, and evolution), SoS model-based engineering, SoS construction and evolution, SoS experience from industrial perspectives and successful case studies; SoS general issues including SoS characterization, quality, development processes and project management; future perspective, challenges, and directions of SoS research and/or development.


  • SECO: SECO platform development, ecosystem architecture, requirements engineering, ecosystem governance, modelling and analysis; business and social aspects of SECO, open source / mobile SECO, developers’ ecosystems, business models and collaboration, empirical studies; future perspective, challenges, and directions of SECO research and/or development.


  • DSD: DSD processes and methods, cloud computing and crowdsourcing, communication and coordination, agile development, business models, project management; social and human aspects of DSD, social network analysis and mining, software evolution and maintenance, open source, security and privacy; future perspective, challenges, and directions of DSD research and/or development.


  • SoS & SECO & DSD: strategies, relationships, and applications of the three topics; technical, economical, and social issues associated to SofEng; reuse-based development; knowledge management; decision-making process; diversity management; sustainability in software systems; case studies, experiments, and industrial experience reports; and systematic reviews and mappings.
