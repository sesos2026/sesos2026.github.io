PAPER SUBMISSION


Two types of submissions are invited:

  • Regular paper (up to seven pages): sound technical contributions from both academia and industry; and
  • Position paper (up to four pages): new ideas, positions, or perspectives of research that are not fully validated.

All papers submitted to the workshop must be unpublished original work and must not be simultaneously under review or submitted elsewhere. All papers will be judged on the basis of their clarity, relevance, originality, and contribution.

All submissions must be in English, formatted according to the ICSE 2016 Format and Submission Guidelines and submitted in either PDF or Postscript format through online upload to the workshop submission website at: https://www.easychair.org/conferences/?conf=sesos2016/.
