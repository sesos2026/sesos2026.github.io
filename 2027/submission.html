Submission Instructions

SESoS 2027 will seek two types of paper submissions:

  • Research papers (up to 8 pages), describing original, substantial contributions in research or practice
  • Short papers (up to 4 pages), including position papers and experience reports describing emerging results, lessons learned, open problems or novel ideas and perspectives on research.

The page limits include abstract, all figures, tables, and references. Submissions do not need to be anonymous.

All submissions must conform to the IEEE conference proceedings template. LaTeX users must use \documentclass[10pt,conference]{IEEEtran} without including the compsoc or compsocconf options. Alterations of spacing, font size, and other changes that deviate from the instructions may result in desk rejection without further review.


Review and Publication Processes

All submitted papers should describe original work and not have been published or under review anywhere. At least three reviewers of the Program Committee will evaluate the papers for clarity, relevance, originality, and contribution.

Accepted papers will be published in IEEE Xplore. The publication of the accepted papers will require the registration of at least one author in ICSE 2027, as well as present the paper in person during the workshop.


Journal Special Issue

Selected high-quality papers presented in SESoS 2027 will be invited to submit extended versions to the Automated Software Engineering for Systems-of-Systems and Software Ecosystems Special Issue of the Automated Software Engineering journal (IF 3.1 (2024), SJR Q2 (2025)).
